# Findings

Data as of 2026-10-31. Window 2020-01-01 onward (see "Why 2020" below). Numbers
regenerated by `analysis/01_demand_performance.py` and `analysis/02_demand_vs_coverage.py`.

## Why 2020

Incident counts step from 241/year (2013-2019 average) to 529/year (2020-2025) — a
2.19x jump in a single year, which is a reporting-practice change, not workload. The
department was volunteer-run with poor logging before that. Within the clean window the
growth is 429 -> 675 incidents, which is defensible.

## 1. Demand is up sharply and still climbing

| Year | Incidents | Per day | YoY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2020 | 429 | 1.18 | — |
| 2021 | 406 | 1.11 | -5% |
| 2022 | 527 | 1.44 | +30% |
| 2023 | 562 | 1.54 | +7% |
| 2024 | 542 | 1.48 | -4% |
| 2025 | 675 | 1.85 | +25% |
| 2026 (Jan-Oct) | 702 | 2.31 | +25% per day |

**+57% from 2020 to 2025, and 2026 is running 25% above 2025 on a per-day basis.**
The 2026 figure covers ten months; September and October are still subject to reporting
lag (see below).

## 2. Response performance has not degraded

In-scope incidents only (no mutual aid, arrival <= 25 min, not wildland):

| Year | n | p50 | p90 | p95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2020 | 344 | 7.3 | 14.7 | 17.1 |
| 2021 | 313 | 6.7 | 13.0 | 15.4 |
| 2022 | 406 | 7.1 | 13.7 | 16.1 |
| 2023 | 433 | 6.9 | 13.8 | 15.4 |
| 2024 | 432 | 7.0 | 13.1 | 15.4 |
| 2025 | 539 | 6.7 | 13.3 | 15.7 |
| 2026 | 346 | 7.0 | 13.5 | 16.2 |

p90 sits between 13.0 and 14.7 minutes in every year of the series. **The department
absorbed a 57% workload increase without getting slower.** This is the strongest finding
in the data and it is robust across seven years.

Caveat: 2026 response times exist for 346 of 702 incidents (49%), because NERIS records
response timestamps far less consistently than the NFIRS era did (89%).

## 3. Time on scene is stable

Unit grain, arrived -> cleared, outliers beyond 4 h excluded:

| Year | Units | p50 | p90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2020 | 592 | 17.0 | 69.2 |
| 2021 | 404 | 18.2 | 77.6 |
| 2022 | 482 | 16.6 | 99.4 |
| 2023 | 532 | 16.9 | 74.7 |
| 2024 | 527 | 14.3 | 67.0 |
| 2025 | 672 | 19.0 | 56.8 |

Median time on scene holds at 14-19 minutes. The p90 is noisier, driven by a handful of
long incidents per year.

## 4. Staffing does not respond to demand

Mean calls per station-day by crew size, 2025-2026:

| Crew on duty | Station-days | Mean calls/day |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 605 | 2.09 |
| 3 | 372 | 2.10 |
| 4+ | 24 | 2.12 |

Essentially flat. Staffing is set by the rotation, and call volume arrives independently
of it — which is exactly why added demand shows up as added load per crew rather than as
scheduling pressure.

## 5. Greenhorn became a staffed station during this period

| Year | GH calls | Share on staffed days | p50 staffed | p50 unstaffed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2025 | 68 | 46% | 7.0 | 7.6 |
| 2026 | 85 | 93% | 7.3 | 10.9 |

Greenhorn went from staffed under half the days to nearly every day, and its call share
grew alongside. The response penalty for an unstaffed day looks small in 2025 (+0.6 min)
but the unstaffed sample is only 43 calls across two years, so this is a lead, not a
conclusion.

## 6. Paramedic availability at Elkhorn (hypothesis, weak sample)

Medical-type incidents attributed to Elkhorn, 2025-2026:

| Paramedic on duty | Calls | p50 | p90 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Yes | 121 | 6.1 | 11.6 |
| No | 16 | 6.3 | 57.0 |

Median response is unchanged, but the p90 without a paramedic is five times higher. With
16 calls this is hypothesis-generating only — it needs a larger window (or the ALS
dispatch flags in First Due) before anyone draws an operational conclusion.

## Caveats that must travel with these numbers

1. **Reporting lag.** The newest months are always incomplete. September-October 2026
   should not be read as decline.
2. **Unit data stops at the NFIRS era.** Only 32 of 693 incidents in 2026 have apparatus
   rows, so time-on-scene and unit-hours are only published through 2025.
3. **Station attribution is the reporting station**, not necessarily the responding one,
   and 36% of 2026 incidents have no station at all.
4. **Jurisdiction is unresolved** for 442 NERIS records; the SVFD/NBCFD split is not
   reportable for 2026.
5. **Coverage data starts 2024-12-31**, so nothing in sections 4-6 can be extended
   backwards.
